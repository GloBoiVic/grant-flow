# GrantFlow

## Product identity

GrantFlow is a grant portfolio management platform for nonprofit organizations. It centralizes grant opportunities, funders, documents, activity history, and portfolio insight.

## Users

- Grant professionals are the primary daily users managing opportunities, deadlines, documents, funder history, and progress.
- Fund development directors are leadership users who need portfolio health, active opportunities, funding projections, and risks.

## Core problem

Grant professionals manage portfolios across disconnected spreadsheets, email, shared drives, calendars, sticky notes, and personal memory. This fragmentation causes missed deadlines, duplicate work, poor pipeline visibility, difficult leadership reporting, and lost institutional knowledge.

## MVP goal

Replace the spreadsheet-based grant tracker with a faster, cleaner, more reliable application that centralizes grant tracking, portfolio visibility, document organization, historical knowledge, and migration from existing spreadsheet data.

## Product boundaries

GrantFlow manages grant opportunities, funders, documents, activity history, and reporting insights. It does not manage donor relationships or fundraising campaigns, accounting or financial operations, AI-generated grant writing, general nonprofit operations, generic project management, or generic file storage.
