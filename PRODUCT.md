# GrantFlow

## Product identity

GrantFlow is a grant portfolio and grant-work management platform for nonprofit organizations. It gives grant professionals one place to manage grant opportunities, funders, deadlines, funding data, working notes, application drafts, documents, activity, revision history, and portfolio insight across the grant lifecycle.

Each grant is a durable workspace for both the structured record and the work required to move that grant forward.

## Users

- Grant professionals are the primary daily users managing grant opportunities, deadlines, funder relationships, application work, notes, submissions, outcomes, and reporting obligations.
- Fund development directors and organizational leadership need clear portfolio health, active opportunities, funding projections, risks, and reviewable grant materials.

The first work-ready deployment may be single-user. Collaboration and leadership accounts are not required for GrantFlow to provide value.

## Core problem

Grant work is fragmented across spreadsheets, Word documents, email, shared drives, calendars, sticky notes, and personal memory.

The spreadsheet may contain the portfolio record while Word contains the proposal, separate notes contain working context, email contains decisions and feedback, and file copies contain competing versions.

This fragmentation creates duplicate work, missed deadlines, poor portfolio visibility, difficult leadership reporting, lost institutional knowledge, and uncertainty about which record or draft is current.

## MVP goal

A grant professional can replace the spreadsheet-based grant tracker with GrantFlow as the reliable source of truth for their active portfolio.

They can bring an existing portfolio into GrantFlow, understand what needs attention, find and update grants, manage deadlines and funders, and work from a complete grant workspace.

Over the grant lifecycle, GrantFlow should also become the canonical home for grant-specific working notes and application drafts, with revision history and export to editable Word and fixed PDF formats for leadership review, feedback, submission, or archival use.

GrantFlow is successful when maintaining a parallel tracking spreadsheet is no longer necessary and grant work can increasingly be completed from the GrantFlow workspace itself.

## Product boundaries

GrantFlow is purpose-built for grant portfolio management and grant work.

It is not a donor CRM or fundraising campaign manager, accounting system, general nonprofit management platform, generic project-management tool, generic file-storage product, or general-purpose word processor.

GrantFlow may provide grant-specific drafting and rich-text capabilities, but it should not attempt to reproduce Microsoft Word as a general document editor.

GrantFlow owns the canonical grant record, working content, and history. Word, PDF, and similar files are review, interchange, submission, or archival formats rather than the primary system of record.

AI-generated grant writing is not required for the core product.
